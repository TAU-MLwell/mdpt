In this cohort of 1,245 adults with hypertension, systolic blood pressure was reduced by 12 mmHg after 6 months of treatment with an ACE inhibitor. The reduction was greater in patients with baseline systolic blood pressure greater than 150 mmHg (mean reduction of 18 mmHg) compared to those with baseline systolic blood pressure between 140 and 150 mmHg (mean reduction of 9 mmHg). No significant difference in response was observed between male and female patients.
